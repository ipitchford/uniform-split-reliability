# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to uniform-split-reliability 1.0.0 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23450265)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v1.0.0` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/uniform-split-reliability/>.

1. Corrected a structural description in §4.3 and in the explanation of Definition 4.4. The archived text says the extremal graph is C_{n−1}(n−3−ε) “with a pendant vertex attached to a clique vertex”. For ε = 1 the pendant is attached to the cone vertex u, the unique vertex of minimum degree. The text should read: “with a pendant vertex attached to a vertex of minimum degree (the cone vertex u when ε = 1; u or its non-neighbour when ε = 0)”, and Definition 4.4 should note that the right side has the same reliability as the balloon, because a pendant edge contributes the factor p wherever it is attached.

   Reliability does not depend on where the pendant edge is attached. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
