# Reproduction

From the repository root, with Python 3, a C compiler and the dependencies in
ENVIRONMENT.txt installed:

```sh
bash verification/reproduce.sh /tmp/umsr-full-fresh
```

Use a new output directory. The full route checks cone orders N=7..50 in normal
and optimized Python, fail-closed command-line controls, loop/zero-star checks,
brute-force root-set checks and 5,422 supplied boundary-case graphs. It compiles
the C implementation from source. It does not execute bundled binaries.

A bounded CI/smoke route is:

```sh
bash verification/reproduce.sh /tmp/umsr-quick-fresh --quick
```

The quick route checks only N=7..10 and is **not** the full finite theorem replay.
Normal and optimized results are retained separately. Expected files include
`cli-{normal,optimized}.log`, `independent-{normal,optimized}.json`,
`rootsets-{normal,optimized}.json`, `base-{normal,optimized}.json`,
`cones/cone-{normal,optimized}.json`, `cone-{normal,optimized}.log` and
`interval-algebra-{normal,optimized}.log`.
Nonzero failure exits and deliberate corrupted/omitted-case controls form part
of the checking contract; a transcript saying PASS alone is not sufficient.

## Historical records and missing large files

The original `logs/` and `results/` are retained historical evidence. Fresh
receipts are identified separately; their exact source hashes and coverage must
be inspected before reuse. Base-case corpus completeness is inherited from the
supplied corpora and documented reviewer regeneration, not independently
regenerated by this release replay. Root-set and coefficient arithmetic checks
do not by themselves certify completeness of an unlabelled graph enumeration.

Historical raw files `out9.txt`, `out11dense.txt` and `out12dense.txt` were not
included in the source bundle. Their historical analyses remain in results/logs.
They can be regenerated by compiling `splitrel.c` and processing the corresponding
graph6 files, but the release must not be read as a fresh replay of all those
large enumerations. Upstream nauty is required only to regenerate graph corpora;
obtain it separately under its upstream terms.

Universal proofs and imported literature theorems are mathematical dependencies,
not certified by the numerical scripts. The analytic interval theorem does not
depend on the finite cone replay; its algebra checks are supplementary evidence.
